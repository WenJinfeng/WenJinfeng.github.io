Academic Homepage

Jinfeng Wen 温 金凤

Assistant Professor · Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications

I am an Assistant Professor at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT), working with Prof. Shangguang Wang. I received my Ph.D. from Peking University (PKU) in July 2024 and was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Xuanzhe Liu. My research interests focus on Software Intelligence in Satellite Computing, Performance Optimization in Serverless Computing, Cloud Computing, and Services Computing.
